What is Burnout?

Burnout is a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress. Our advanced detection system uses multiple analysis methods to help identify early signs of burnout, enabling you to take proactive steps toward better mental health and work-life balance. Whether through detailed questionnaires, voice pattern analysis, or facial expression recognition, we provide comprehensive insights into your well-being.
